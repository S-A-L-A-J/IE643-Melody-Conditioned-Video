We extended the preserved music-conditioned GRU predictor using a rolling four-second context, producing clearer eight- and sixteen-second validation videos without retraining the predictor. On one fixed validation recording, sixteen-second estimated-beat F1 increased from 0.918 under direct recurrent extrapolation to 0.968 with rolling context. We also evaluated native-GPU AnimateDiff refinement using rendered-video initialization, finer low-strength schedules and overlapping denoising windows for longer sequences. A bounded 128-update spatial LoRA reduced fixed eight-recording validation denoising loss from 0.294 to 0.145, but a generated-video improvement over the predictor was not established; at 64 updates, refinement remained worse in target-latent MSE than predictor-only output. We therefore retained the longer predictor and stopped further diffusion experiments. Audio control enters through the GRU; the adapter learns synthetic visual appearance. Estimated-beat metrics do not establish semantic quality, mood accuracy or human preference.
