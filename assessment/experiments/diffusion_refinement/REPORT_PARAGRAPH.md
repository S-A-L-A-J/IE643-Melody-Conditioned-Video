We evaluated frozen SD1.5–AnimateDiff latent refinement initialized from the improved music-conditioned GRU predictor on one fixed four-second validation clip. With matched seed, noise, text and a 20-step DDIM base schedule, strengths 0.10, 0.20 and 0.30 preserved estimated-beat F1 at 0.80 but introduced progressively distorted rings; flow-warp residual increased from 0.0204 to 0.0248, 0.0355 and 0.0474. Wrong-recording and shifted-feature controls at strength 0.10 retained their predictor timing scores, consistent with control inherited through the initial latents. We therefore rejected this refinement configuration and retained the predictor-only generator. Diffusion was frozen and received no audio, so this was not audio-conditioned diffusion training. The single-clip CPU pilot and estimated-beat metrics do not establish semantic quality, mood accuracy or human preference.
